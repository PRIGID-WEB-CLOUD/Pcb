## Brand & Style

The design system is anchored in a philosophy of **Modern Minimalism** with an **Editorial** flair. It aims to evoke a sense of curated luxury and absolute reliability. By prioritizing high-contrast structural elements and generous whitespace, the interface recedes to let product photography take center stage. The aesthetic is "quiet luxury"—sophisticated, intentional, and clutter-free, ensuring the user feels they are browsing a high-end flagship boutique rather than a high-volume marketplace.

## Colors

The palette is built on a foundation of Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) to establish authority and trust, replacing standard blacks for a softer, more sophisticated look. Emerald Green (`#10B981`) serves as a vibrant, high-energy accent for primary actions and success states, symbolizing growth and vitality. 

Backgrounds utilize varying shades of off-white and cool greys to maintain a clean environment, while text scales from deep charcoal to muted slate to establish clear visual hierarchy.

## Typography

This design system employs a sophisticated pairing of **Noto Serif** for headings and **Manrope** for functional text. 

- **Noto Serif** provides the editorial "magazine" feel, used for product titles and section headers to convey heritage and quality.
- **Manrope** is used for its geometric clarity and exceptional legibility at small sizes, handling all utility, navigation, and body copy.
- Use **Label-Caps** for category tags or small eyebrow text above headlines to add a structured, professional touch.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** model for desktop, centered within a 1280px container to ensure a focused browsing experience. 

- **Rhythm:** A strict 8px base unit governs all dimensions.
- **Whitespace:** Emphasize "Ample Whitespace." Product cards and sections should have significant breathing room (`section-padding`) to prevent the interface from feeling crowded.
- **Grid:** A 12-column system is used, with generous 24px gutters to reinforce the premium, airy aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is achieved through **Ambient Shadows** and subtle tonal layering rather than harsh borders. 

- **Low Elevation:** Used for cards and input fields. Use a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)) to lift elements off the background.
- **High Elevation:** Used for floating carts and modals. A more pronounced, deep shadow (0px 10px 40px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)) provides clear focus.
- **Tonal Tiers:** Use the tertiary color (`#F8FAFC`) to differentiate the header or footer area from the main content body without needing a structural line.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft** but disciplined. 
- A standard radius of `4px` (0.25rem) is applied to buttons, input fields, and small UI components to maintain a modern, clean look.
- Product cards and large containers may use `rounded-lg` (8px) for a gentler feel. 
- Avoid fully rounded "pill" shapes for primary buttons to maintain the formal, trustworthy tone of the store.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons use the Deep Navy background with white text. Hover states should transition smoothly to the Emerald Green accent. Secondary buttons use a ghost style with a subtle 1px border.

### Form Fields
Inputs feature a light grey background and a 1px inset border. On focus, the border transitions to Deep Navy with a soft glow effect. Error states should use a muted coral rather than a bright red to maintain the palette's sophistication.

### Product Cards
Cards are borderless, relying on a subtle ambient shadow and whitespace. The product image should have a slight zoom-in effect on hover to add interactivity without cluttering the UI.

### Navigation
The navigation should be sticky and minimal. Use the `label-caps` typography for top-level links to create a sense of order.

### Success/Feedback
Use the Emerald Green accent for price tags, "In Stock" indicators, and successful checkout notifications to create positive reinforcement.
